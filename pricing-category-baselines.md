## Sources

Jewelry ranges were anchored by Forbes’ reported online jewelry price range of approximately $40 to $6,500+, and Nordstrom’s 14k-gold lariat listings, which included products from roughly $395 into the thousands. Sources: [Forbes jewelry stores](https://www.forbes.com/sites/forbes-personal-shopper/article/best-online-jewelry-store/) and [Nordstrom 14k-gold lariats](https://www.nordstrom.com/browse/women/jewelry/necklaces/lariat-necklaces?filterByMaterial=14k-gold).

Luxury appliance ranges were anchored by Aurora Circ’s high-end appliance catalog, which displayed list prices from approximately $1,199 to over $7,000, with some products above $13,000. Source: [Aurora Circ luxury appliances](https://auroracirc.com/collections/all-appliances).

Consumer-electronics pricing was anchored by OpenBrand’s reported $463 industry average price paid for consumer electronics, with the premium band set above that average. Source: [OpenBrand consumer electronics retail data](https://openbrand.com/newsroom/blog/consumer-electronics-market-top-brands-retailers-retail-sales-data-trends).

Collectibles and entertainment categories were anchored by Entertainment Earth’s product scope covering toys, action figures, collectibles, licensed products, exclusives, and preorders. Source: [Entertainment Earth](https://www.entertainmentearth.com/).

Google Merchant Center requires sale price data to match the landing page and checkout, and requires the sale price to be lower than the regular price. Source: [Google Sale Price](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324471) and [Google Checkout Requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9158778).
