Operating rules

  • Only mission-supported products and required locations are used.
  • Every job is isolated by product, location, platform and job ID.
  • CAPTCHA, login, verification and consent checkpoints require human action.
  • No CAPTCHA solving, bypassing, replaying, prediction, or anti-bot defeat is performed.
  • An ad is not considered live until the posting integration returns a usable public URL.
  • Only verified URLs are synchronized to Market / Active Ads.
  • Failures and non-live results remain documented for resubmission instead of being represented as successful ads.
