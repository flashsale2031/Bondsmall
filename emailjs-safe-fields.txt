EmailJS safe fields:
order_id
customer_full_name
customer_email
customer_phone
shipping_address_formatted
order_items_detailed
order_subtotal
order_tax_amount
order_final_total
payment_method_type
payment_card_type
card_last4
payment_reference
payment_status
order_status

Forbidden in EmailJS:
card_number / PAN
card_cvv / CVV
card_expiry
Visa username/password
Visa certificate/private key
MLE key ID/shared secret
session/access tokens
bank account credentials
SSN or identity documents
