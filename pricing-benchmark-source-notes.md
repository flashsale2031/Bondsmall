# Bondsmall benchmark pricing source notes

## Google Merchant Center constraints

Google’s official sale-price guidance states that the submitted sale price must match the sale price shown on the landing page and at checkout, that the regular price must also be submitted, and that the sale price must be lower than the regular price. Source: [Google Merchant Center Sale Price](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/6324471).

Google’s checkout guidance states that the advertised price should remain consistent through the checkout process. Source: [Google Merchant Center Checkout Requirements](https://support.google.com/merchants/answer/9158778).
