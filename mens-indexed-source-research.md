# Men’s indexed-source research

A broad Bing query for men’s clothing product galleries returned mostly film and general-definition results rather than product pages. The next discovery pass must use retailer/category-specific queries and exact product terms, then inspect product pages for four or more gallery assets. Generic search thumbnails and category pages are not acceptable sources.

Yahoo targeted search for “men's shirt product retailer” returned a men's-shirt category page and image-search thumbnails rather than individual product pages. Those URLs are rejected for catalog ingestion; future queries must target exact product names or retailer product URLs and verify a four-image gallery on the product page.

Yahoo exact-query research found the official Ralph Lauren page **Men's Polo Iconic Oxford Shirt** at https://www.ralphlauren.com/men-clothing-button-down-shirts/the-iconic-oxford-shirt---all-fits/342680-C.html. Yahoo’s result included a product-specific title, official brand page, and a multi-image panel; individual image-result links also exposed product image URLs from Ralph Lauren and retailer/indexed sources. This is a candidate for manual four-image validation, not yet an imported catalog record.

Rendered-browser validation of the Ralph Lauren page confirmed the official product gallery is client-rendered and exposes distinct product views for product asset set `s7-1151197`: main/alternate10, lifestyle, alternate1, alternate3, alternate4, alternate5, and alternate20. Responsive mobile/zoom variants were deduplicated; the candidate therefore exceeds the four-image requirement. The page displays “The Iconic Oxford Shirt - All Fits,” Polo Ralph Lauren, with official price $130.00 and a sale/variation price of $89.99. The product page URL remains the manufacturer source; image URLs are official Ralph Lauren Scene7 assets.

Yahoo/Bing-indexed official Ralph Lauren page **Men’s Soft Cotton Polo Shirt – All Fits**: https://www.ralphlauren.com/men-clothing-polo-shirts/soft-cotton-polo-shirt---all-fits/401482-P.html. The page displays Polo Ralph Lauren and $118.00, with a lower variation price shown in the rendered view. Browser DOM extraction exposed only two distinct non-swatch product views so far (`alternate10` and `lifestyle` for asset set `AI710660606194`); color swatches are not accepted as gallery images. This candidate is rejected for import until at least four distinct product views are verified.

The official Ralph Lauren Iconic Mesh Polo page (https://www.ralphlauren.com/men-clothing-polo-shirts/the-iconic-mesh-polo-shirt---all-fits/401480-P.html) presented a human-verification challenge during this pass, and the indexed Nordstrom product page (https://www.nordstrom.com/s/polo-ralph-lauren-the-iconic-cotton-mesh-polo/8639676) returned Forbidden. Neither source was imported because the product-specific gallery could not be verified. Search results also surfaced Ralph Lauren hidden product endpoints, but those require the same access validation before use.
