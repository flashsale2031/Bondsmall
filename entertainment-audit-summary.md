# Entertainment imagery audit

The Entertainment category contains 21,218 products across 1,949 catalog chunks. The first visible curated records include many wide lifestyle/banner images (for example 1280x720 and 2048x810 assets), external marketplace URLs, and galleries containing only two images, which explains the close-cropped or barely visible product presentation.

The first curated group spans IDs 24, 48, 50, 60, 125, 128, 152, 157, 158, and 159, followed by IDs 221–240. Eleven Xbox, RIG, Lexibook, and G5 records were already corrected in the previous update. A clean product-only Turtle Beach Recon Cloud controller asset has now also been downloaded as `assets/manufacturer-images/222-corrected.jpg`.

Because the category has more than twenty-one thousand records, a complete product-only replacement requires a defined scope or a source-based batch policy. The practical first batch is the curated first 40–60 Entertainment records, where the image defects are visible and the products have identifiable retailer or manufacturer source pages.
