1. Scope, hierarchy, and interpretation

This rulebook is a governance guide for the way Bonds Mall conducts commerce, operates software, communicates with participants, handles information, processes orders, and resolves concerns. It should be read with the published Terms of Service, Privacy Notice, Returns and Refunds Policy, Shipping Policy, Seller Rules, payment-provider terms, and any order-specific terms.

  1. Mandatory law and lawful orders of competent authorities take priority.
  2. Signed or expressly accepted contracts take priority over general operational guidance to the extent of a conflict.
  3. Consumer, privacy, accessibility, employment, tax, customs, product-safety, and payment protections must not be reduced by a website notice.
  4. Where a rule is unclear, the responsible team should pause the affected action, document the question, and obtain qualified legal or compliance review.

2. Universal principles

At the universal level, every Bonds Mall action should be guided by human dignity, honesty, fairness, safety, accountability, accessibility, privacy, and respect for lawful rights. No person may be targeted, excluded, deceived, threatened, harassed, or disadvantaged because of a protected characteristic or because they raised a good-faith concern.

  • Integrity: records, listings, prices, reviews, communications, and business reports must be accurate and not knowingly misleading.
  • Non-harm: the marketplace must not knowingly facilitate fraud, abuse, trafficking, exploitation, dangerous goods, or unlawful discrimination.
  • Accountability: decisions should have an identifiable owner, a documented reason, and a review path appropriate to the risk.
  • Proportionality: controls, suspensions, data requests, and enforcement should be no broader than reasonably necessary.
  • Accessibility: people should be able to access essential information and support through reasonable, legally compliant accommodations.

3. Intercontinental and international framework

When Bonds Mall serves or communicates with people across borders, the business should identify the relevant countries, currencies, languages, taxes, customs requirements, trade restrictions, consumer rights, privacy rules, and product-safety obligations before execution.

  • Do not transact with sanctioned parties, prohibited destinations, or restricted goods where applicable law forbids the transaction.
  • Present material prices, shipping charges, taxes, currency conversions, delivery estimates, and import responsibilities clearly before purchase where required.
  • Use appropriate cross-border data-transfer safeguards and do not move personal information without a lawful basis and required notices.
  • Preserve records needed for customs, tax, anti-money-laundering, payment, and consumer-protection compliance, subject to retention limits.
  • Use translators and automated localization as convenience tools, not as a substitute for legally required language, notice, or accessibility review.

5. National, state, provincial, and local framework

Each market may have its own requirements for business registration, sales and use taxes, VAT or GST, consumer contracts, returns, shipping, employment, wages, accessibility, privacy, intellectual property, packaging, warranties, and product restrictions. Bonds Mall teams must not assume that a rule valid in one jurisdiction is valid everywhere.

Before launching a market, the responsible owner should document the legal entity, contracting party, governing terms, required notices, tax treatment, payment rules, customer support route, return address, delivery commitments, and escalation authority. If a conflict exists, the stricter lawful customer-protection rule should be followed pending counsel review.

6. Company governance

Bonds Mall company governance should separate commercial approval, financial control, technical access, customer support, content moderation, and compliance review where practical. No employee or contractor may use company authority for personal gain, conceal a material error, falsify a record, retaliate against a reporter, or approve a transaction outside their delegated authority.

  • Maintain written ownership for policies, product data, pricing, promotions, payments, fulfillment, privacy, security, and incidents.
  • Keep an audit trail for material catalog changes, refunds, account actions, access grants, listing removals, and legal requests.
  • Protect company credentials, tokens, customer records, supplier information, source code, and confidential business information.
  • Disclose conflicts of interest and recuse decision-makers when independence could reasonably be questioned.
  • Use approval thresholds for high-risk products, unusual discounts, bulk changes, external listings, and irreversible account or payment actions.

7. Employee, contractor, and partner guide

Employees, contractors, agencies, and partners must act professionally, follow lawful instructions, protect confidential information, and use systems only for authorized business purposes. They must not discriminate, harass, threaten, manipulate customers, misuse personal data, interfere with investigations, or retaliate against someone who reports a concern.

Work assignments should be performed with reasonable care and documented handoffs. A person who discovers a security incident, unsafe product, suspected fraud, privacy issue, material pricing error, or legal complaint should preserve relevant evidence and report it promptly through the designated internal channel.

8. Customer, seller, and merchant guide

Customers and sellers must provide truthful account, order, payment, shipping, product, and identity information. Sellers are responsible for having the rights, permits, inventory, quality, authenticity, safety information, and fulfillment capability required for the goods they offer.

  • Listings must accurately describe the product, condition, brand, quantity, images, specifications, availability, price, shipping, and applicable restrictions.
  • Customers must not use stolen payment instruments, create deceptive accounts, abuse returns or chargebacks, manipulate reviews, or submit knowingly false claims.
  • Sellers must not counterfeit goods, infringe intellectual property, evade platform controls, sell prohibited products, or move transactions off-platform to defeat safety or payment controls.
  • Disputes should be raised promptly with order details and supporting evidence so the appropriate team can investigate fairly.

9. General user and visitor conduct

Visitors may browse public content lawfully and respectfully. Users must not interfere with availability, probe or bypass security, scrape in a way that violates published limits or law, upload malware, impersonate another person, harvest personal information, submit unlawful content, or attempt to manipulate product rankings, prices, reviews, or account systems.

Account credentials are personal to the account holder. Users should use strong, unique credentials, keep contact information current, and report suspected compromise. Bonds Mall may restrict access when reasonably necessary to protect people, systems, transactions, or legal rights, with a review route where appropriate.

10. Business execution and practice

Product, category, and catalog operations

Catalog teams must use reliable sources, preserve product identity, avoid misleading substitutions, keep category assignments defensible, and maintain appropriate image and specification records. Similar-product fallback is permitted only when clearly identified and not presented as the original product.

Pricing, promotions, and advertising

Prices and promotions must be truthful, mathematically accurate, consistently applied, and supported by records. A crossed-out or reference price must not be fabricated. Material conditions, limits, expiration dates, shipping charges, taxes, subscriptions, and eligibility rules should be disclosed before commitment.

Orders, fulfillment, returns, and support

Order status, delivery estimates, stock information, cancellations, refunds, returns, warranties, and customer communications must be handled according to the applicable published policy and mandatory law. Staff should not promise an outcome they do not control.

11. E-commerce

This E-commerce section governs the online sale, purchase, listing, promotion, payment, delivery, return, support, and administration of products offered through Bonds Mall. It applies to the marketplace, direct sales, seller listings, affiliates, promotional channels, customer accounts, guest transactions, payment services, and connected commerce tools. It operates together with applicable law, the Privacy Notice, Returns and Refunds Policy, Shipping Policy, Seller Rules, payment-provider terms, and the order-specific terms shown before checkout.

Policy clause

Bonds Mall’s e-commerce policy is to conduct commerce honestly, safely, transparently, and with reasonable care for customers, sellers, employees, partners, communities, and the environment. Policies are designed to support lawful trade and consistent service; they do not reduce a right or protection that cannot lawfully be waived.

11.1 Product and listing policy

  • Every listing should identify the product accurately, including the commercial name, brand where known, condition, materials, dimensions, quantity, model or identifier, included items, limitations, images, and material specifications.
  • Images must represent the listed product or be clearly identified as illustrative. Similar-product images, substitutions, mockups, stock photos, and generated images must not be presented as proof of an exact item when they are not.
  • Listings must not contain counterfeit goods, stolen goods, deceptive replicas, unsafe products, unlawful services, prohibited financial instruments, or products restricted by applicable law or marketplace policy.
  • Product categories, attributes, brands, availability, and shipping origin should be maintained accurately so customers can make informed choices and safety notices can reach affected buyers.
  • Sellers must have the right to offer the product and must preserve evidence of authenticity, sourcing, safety, licenses, permits, and supply-chain traceability when applicable.

11.2 Pricing and promotion policy

  • Prices, reference prices, discounts, coupon codes, bundles, subscriptions, taxes, shipping charges, and other fees must be calculated and displayed accurately.
  • A reference or crossed-out price must be genuine, supportable, and presented in a manner that does not create a false impression of savings.
  • Promotions must state material conditions, eligibility, geographic limits, inventory limits, expiration dates, exclusions, and any required redemption steps.
  • Bonds Mall may correct an obvious typographical, data, currency, or calculation error and may cancel or re-price an affected order where permitted by law, with prompt notice and a refund of amounts paid for a cancelled order.
  • Prices may vary by market, currency, tax, seller, inventory, delivery destination, or promotion. The checkout total and applicable terms control for the specific order.

11.3 Advertising, content, and reviews policy

  • Commercial claims should be truthful, supportable, current, and understandable to the intended audience.
  • Paid placements, affiliate links, sponsored content, influencer relationships, comparative claims, endorsements, urgency claims, and customer incentives must be identified where required.
  • Reviews and ratings must reflect genuine experiences. Users may not purchase reviews, suppress material negative feedback, impersonate reviewers, or coordinate manipulation.
  • Users grant only the rights necessary for Bonds Mall to host, display, moderate, and operate with submitted content, subject to the applicable content terms and privacy rules.
  • Do not submit content that infringes intellectual property, violates privacy, contains malware, exposes confidential information, or is unlawful, hateful, harassing, threatening, or abusive.

11.4 Account and identity policy

  • Account information must be accurate, current, and controlled by the person or organization using the account.
  • Users must protect credentials, payment instruments, recovery methods, and authentication codes and must report suspected compromise promptly.
  • One person may not impersonate another person, create deceptive duplicate accounts, evade a restriction, or use another person’s account without authorization.
  • Bonds Mall may request proportionate verification for fraud prevention, age or eligibility checks, seller onboarding, payment security, sanctions screening, or legal compliance.
  • Account restrictions should be documented and reviewed through an appropriate support or appeal route when feasible and legally appropriate.

11.5 Ordering and contract-formation policy

  • A customer should review the item, quantity, price, delivery address, taxes, shipping cost, return terms, and final total before submitting an order.
  • Adding an item to a cart or submitting an order request does not guarantee inventory, price, acceptance, or delivery.
  • An order is accepted only when Bonds Mall or the applicable seller confirms acceptance under the displayed checkout terms. Automated acknowledgments may confirm receipt without confirming acceptance.
  • Orders may be held, declined, limited, or cancelled for lack of stock, payment failure, fraud indicators, address restrictions, legal restrictions, supplier failure, system error, or other lawful business reasons.
  • Where an order is cancelled after payment, the responsible party should initiate a refund or reversal through the original payment method as required by the applicable policy and law.

11.6 Payment and financial-transaction policy

  • Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Bonds Mall should not store full payment credentials when a secure provider can process them instead.
  • Customers may use only payment methods they are authorized to use and must not submit stolen, altered, deceptive, or unauthorized payment information.
  • Payment authorization, capture, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, currency conversion, and financial records may be subject to provider rules and timing outside Bonds Mall’s direct control.
  • Bonds Mall may use reasonable transaction monitoring and verification to reduce fraud, money laundering, account takeover, abuse, and unlawful payments.
  • Customers should contact Bonds Mall before initiating a chargeback when practical, but no policy requires a customer to surrender a lawful right to dispute an unauthorized or improper charge.

11.7 Tax, customs, and cross-border policy

  • Taxes, duties, customs charges, brokerage fees, import restrictions, and delivery requirements may depend on the customer’s location, product, seller, and shipment route.
  • Where required, Bonds Mall or the seller should provide tax information, invoices, receipts, customs data, and legally required seller or importer details.
  • Customers are responsible for providing an accurate delivery destination and for cooperating with lawful customs or delivery requests. The checkout page should identify charges or responsibilities known at the time of order.
  • Cross-border goods must not be shipped to prohibited destinations or in violation of export, import, sanctions, product-safety, or trade-control requirements.

11.8 Shipping, fulfillment, and delivery policy

  • Shipping methods, origins, delivery estimates, tracking availability, handling times, and applicable restrictions should be described accurately.
  • Delivery estimates are estimates unless a binding delivery commitment is expressly stated. Delays may arise from inventory, carriers, weather, customs, public emergencies, address issues, or events outside reasonable control.
  • Sellers and fulfillment partners must package products appropriately, use the agreed carrier or method, provide accurate tracking where available, and respond to lost, damaged, or misdelivered shipment reports.
  • Customers should inspect deliveries promptly, preserve packaging and evidence for damage claims, and report material delivery problems within the period stated in the applicable policy or required by law.
  • Risk of loss, title, delivery responsibility, and signature requirements depend on the order terms, seller arrangement, shipping method, and applicable law.

11.9 Returns, refunds, cancellations, and warranties policy

  • Returns, cancellations, refunds, exchanges, credits, warranties, and repair remedies are governed by the published policy, the product listing, seller terms, and mandatory law.
  • Return eligibility may depend on product type, condition, timing, customization, hygiene, digital delivery, safety restrictions, final-sale disclosure, or other lawful limits disclosed before purchase.
  • Refunds should identify the amount, method, timing, deductions, shipping treatment, and any required return steps. A refund is not complete until the payment provider or financial institution processes it.
  • Customers must not abuse return, refund, warranty, replacement, or chargeback processes through false claims, serial misuse, product substitution, or concealment of damage.
  • Sellers must honor valid warranties, statutory guarantees, recalls, and remedies that apply to their products and jurisdiction.

11.10 Marketplace seller and partner policy

  • Sellers and partners are responsible for their legal identity, tax registrations, permissions, inventory, product compliance, customer-facing disclosures, fulfillment, and lawful treatment of customer information.
  • Sellers must not move a Bondsmall transaction off-platform to evade fees, records, safety review, payment controls, or dispute handling.
  • Partners may not use Bonds Mall data, brand assets, customer lists, product feeds, APIs, or credentials outside the permissions and contract that authorize their use.
  • Bonds Mall may request documents, suspend a listing, hold funds where permitted, or terminate a relationship when information is incomplete, unreliable, unsafe, unlawful, or materially misleading.
  • Nothing in this policy creates an employment, agency, franchise, fiduciary, joint-venture, or exclusive relationship unless a separate written agreement expressly does so.

11.11 Privacy, security, and accessibility policy

  • Personal information should be collected, used, shared, retained, and deleted according to the Privacy Notice, disclosed purposes, user choices, contracts, and applicable law.
  • Teams should use data minimization, access controls, encryption or equivalent safeguards, secure development, vendor review, monitoring, backups, and incident response appropriate to the risk.
  • Customer data must not be used for unrelated marketing, profiling, resale, or disclosure without an appropriate legal basis, notice, consent, contract, or other authorization.
  • Bonds Mall should provide reasonable accessibility support and avoid interfaces, documents, or communications that unnecessarily prevent people from understanding or using essential commerce functions.
  • Security reports should be directed to Customer Service or the designated security contact and should not include unnecessary personal or confidential information.

11.12 Prohibited-commerce policy

Users must not use Bonds Mall to conduct, facilitate, promote, or conceal unlawful or harmful activity. Prohibited activity includes fraud, identity theft, unauthorized payments, counterfeiting, trafficking, stolen goods, weapons or unsafe goods where prohibited, controlled substances, exploitation, money laundering, sanctions evasion, harassment, hate-based abuse, malicious code, deceptive advertising, review manipulation, market manipulation, and attempts to compromise systems or accounts.

11.13 Support, complaints, and dispute policy

  • Customers should provide the order number, relevant dates, product information, requested remedy, and concise supporting evidence when contacting support.
  • Support teams should acknowledge material complaints, preserve records, communicate next steps, avoid retaliation, and escalate safety, privacy, payment, legal, and accessibility issues to the responsible team.
  • Dispute decisions should be based on the listing, order record, communications, payment status, tracking, evidence, applicable policy, and mandatory law.
  • Customers retain any non-waivable rights to contact regulators, payment providers, consumer-protection bodies, courts, arbitrators, or other lawful dispute forums.

  1. The user represents that they are legally able to enter the applicable transaction or are acting with the authority of the person or organization that will be bound.
  2. Minors may use commerce services only with the involvement and authorization of a parent or legal guardian where required.
  3. Business users represent that their account, purchases, listings, and instructions are authorized by the business and comply with its internal and legal obligations.
  4. Bonds Mall may refuse access or require additional verification when eligibility, identity, authority, safety, fraud, or legal compliance cannot reasonably be confirmed.

11.16 Listings, product information, and availability

  1. The seller is responsible for the accuracy, legality, quality, safety, authenticity, and fulfillment of its listing, except to the extent Bonds Mall expressly assumes a separate responsibility.
  2. Catalog information may be updated, corrected, translated, localized, or reformatted for display, but material errors should be corrected promptly.
  3. Availability is not guaranteed until the order is accepted. A product shown as available may become unavailable because of simultaneous sales, inventory errors, supplier issues, or legal restrictions.
  4. Descriptions and images are informational and should not be treated as professional, medical, safety, financial, or technical advice unless expressly identified and legally supported.

11.17 Orders, acceptance, and cancellation

  1. The customer authorizes Bonds Mall or its payment provider to process the disclosed amount when the customer submits an order and payment is authorized.
  2. An order acknowledgment confirms receipt only. Acceptance occurs when the applicable seller or Bonds Mall confirms the order under the displayed terms, unless mandatory law provides otherwise.
  3. Bonds Mall may cancel or limit an order for the policy reasons listed above and will provide notice and any required refund or remedy.
  4. A customer may cancel an order only through the available cancellation route and within the applicable window, except where a broader statutory right applies.
  5. Partial acceptance, back-ordering, substitutions, or split shipment may occur only where clearly disclosed or affirmatively agreed.

11.18 Payment, pricing, and fees

  1. The customer must pay the final amount displayed at checkout, including applicable product prices, discounts, taxes, shipping, duties, and fees.
  2. Displayed currency conversions may be estimates until the payment provider or issuing institution completes the transaction.
  3. Unauthorized, reversed, disputed, or fraudulent payments do not discharge the customer’s obligation for goods lawfully received, subject to applicable consumer rights and resolution procedures.
  4. Sellers are responsible for fees, commissions, taxes, invoices, and payout information stated in their seller agreement or applicable marketplace terms.

11.19 Delivery, inspection, and acceptance

  1. The customer must provide a deliverable address and reasonable delivery instructions and must be available to receive age-restricted, signature-required, or otherwise controlled goods.
  2. The customer should inspect the package and product within a reasonable period and promptly report damage, shortage, wrong item, or material nonconformity.
  3. Failure to report an issue immediately does not remove a mandatory legal right, but may affect the investigation of facts, carrier claims, or voluntary policy remedies.
  4. Delivery may be completed by a carrier, pickup location, authorized agent, or other method stated at checkout.

11.20 Returns, refunds, warranties, and remedies

  1. Return instructions, time limits, condition requirements, exclusions, and shipping responsibilities are stated in the applicable returns policy and order terms.
  2. Refund timing begins when the responsible party authorizes the refund and may depend on carrier receipt, inspection, payment-provider processing, and financial-institution posting.
  3. Where a product is defective, unsafe, materially different, counterfeit, or not delivered, the customer may have remedies under mandatory law in addition to any voluntary Bonds Mall policy.
  4. Remedies must not be limited in a manner prohibited by applicable law. Any limitation is subject to statutory rights and the applicable contract.

11.21 User content, intellectual property, and permissions

  1. The user retains rights they own in submitted content but grants Bonds Mall the limited, non-exclusive permission reasonably necessary to host, reproduce, adapt for technical display, moderate, and show that content in connection with the service.
  2. The user represents that they have the rights and permissions needed for their submitted content and that it does not violate another person’s privacy, publicity, intellectual-property, confidentiality, or other rights.
  3. Bonds Mall may remove, restrict, preserve, or disclose content when reasonably necessary for safety, legal compliance, investigation, service operation, or enforcement.
  4. Bonds Mall names, logos, designs, software, and service materials may not be copied, modified, reverse engineered, or used commercially without permission except as allowed by law.

11.22 Prohibited use and enforcement

  1. The user must not bypass rate limits, access controls, fraud controls, geographic restrictions, age controls, payment safeguards, or account restrictions.
  2. The user must not scrape, crawl, harvest, reverse engineer, load-test, disrupt, or automate the service in a way that violates law, published rules, or reasonable technical limits.
  3. The user must not upload malware, use another person’s credentials, harvest customer information, manipulate rankings or reviews, or use the service to facilitate an unlawful transaction.
  4. Bonds Mall may warn, moderate, limit, suspend, terminate, cancel, withhold a listing, preserve evidence, or refer a matter to an appropriate authority where permitted by law.
  5. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the risk and should not be retaliatory or discriminatory.

11.23 Disclaimers and liability framework

  1. The service may contain interruptions, delays, errors, third-party links, carrier failures, payment-provider outages, translation errors, or information that requires correction.
  2. Except where a separate written warranty or mandatory law applies, Bonds Mall does not promise that every listing, seller, product, delivery estimate, translation, price, or service feature will always be available, error-free, or suitable for a particular purpose.
  3. No exclusion or limitation applies to liability that cannot lawfully be excluded or limited, including rights arising from fraud, intentional misconduct, certain personal injury, statutory consumer protections, or other mandatory rules.
  4. Where permitted by law, a party’s recoverable damages and remedies may be limited to the direct losses reasonably attributable to the breach and the amount paid or payable for the affected transaction, subject to any stronger protection required by law.

11.24 Privacy, communications, and electronic notices

  1. The user consents to receive transactional notices, receipts, order updates, security alerts, and support communications electronically where lawful and technically available.
  2. Marketing communications require the consent or other lawful basis required by applicable law and should include a functional opt-out where required.
  3. The Privacy Notice explains how personal information is handled and forms part of the user’s commerce relationship to the extent stated there.
  4. The user must keep contact and delivery information current. Notices sent to the most recent valid contact information may be treated as delivered where permitted by law.

11.25 Disputes, governing law, and regional safeguards

  1. Participants should first use Customer Service and the Dispute Center to seek an efficient resolution, without giving up a lawful right to use a regulator, payment provider, court, arbitration, or other forum.
  2. The applicable order terms or service terms must identify governing law, venue, arbitration or court rules, notice procedures, and any required consumer or small-claims protections.
  3. Mandatory laws of the customer’s jurisdiction may apply regardless of a general governing-law clause. Nothing in these terms is intended to select a forum or law where that selection is prohibited.
  4. Cross-border users are responsible for complying with lawful customs, import, export, tax, and local product requirements applicable to their transaction.

11.26 Changes, severability, and survival

  1. Bonds Mall may update these policies and terms to reflect service, operational, security, or legal changes. Material changes should be versioned and communicated through an appropriate channel.
  2. If a provision is invalid or unenforceable, it should be modified to the minimum extent necessary or severed without invalidating the remaining provisions, subject to applicable law.
  3. Provisions concerning payment obligations, records, intellectual property, privacy, dispute resolution, limitations, investigations, and accrued rights may survive completion or termination of a transaction to the extent appropriate.
  4. The current version, order-specific disclosures, and applicable mandatory law together determine the terms governing a particular transaction.

12. System process, security, and structure

  1. Design: build access control, validation, audit logging, availability, privacy, and recovery into material systems.
  2. Change management: review code, catalog, pricing, policy, and infrastructure changes; preserve rollback information for high-impact changes.
  3. Data lifecycle: collect, use, share, retain, and delete personal information according to documented purpose and applicable law.
  4. Incident response: identify, contain, preserve evidence, assess impact, notify responsible stakeholders, and document remediation.
  5. Business continuity: maintain backups, restore procedures, alternative communications, and clear ownership for service interruptions.
  6. Third parties: evaluate providers, permissions, data access, security commitments, service limits, and exit procedures before integration.

13. Reporting, investigations, and enforcement

Good-faith reports of suspected fraud, safety concerns, harassment, discrimination, privacy violations, security vulnerabilities, intellectual-property infringement, or unlawful activity should be reviewed without retaliation. Reports should contain enough detail to investigate, but reporters should avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Depending on the facts and applicable law, responses may include education, correction, listing removal, refund review, account restrictions, suspension, termination, preservation of evidence, vendor action, regulator notification, or referral to law enforcement. Enforcement should be documented, proportionate, and subject to internal review where feasible.

Disputes and governing terms

Participants should first use the published customer-support and dispute-center processes. Formal dispute terms, governing law, venue, arbitration or court provisions, consumer rights, and notice requirements must be stated in the applicable contract and localized where required; this guide does not replace those terms.

Bonds Mall may update this guide to reflect operational, technical, or legal changes. Material changes should be versioned and communicated through an appropriate channel. Continued use of a service is not, by itself, a substitute for any consent or notice required by law.
