This governance establishes a common operating framework for the lawful, fair, secure, and accountable execution of Bonds Mall business. It is designed as a guide for the marketplace, its company functions, employees, contractors, customers, sellers, affiliates, visitors, and users. Applicable law, binding contracts, mandatory consumer protections, and valid regulatory requirements control where they differ from this guide.

This governance applies a four-level legal framework to every section. Universal: human dignity, equality, privacy, safety, property, expression, effective remedy, and due process principles reflected in the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Intercontinental: cross-border conflicts of law, customs, sanctions, tax, consumer, data-transfer, and trade rules applicable when a transaction, person, service, or data flow crosses regions or continents. International: treaties, conventions, and international or regional instruments, including the ICCPR, GDPR, and other instruments adopted or implemented by the relevant States. National: the constitutions, statutes, regulations, cases, licenses, orders, and mandatory consumer, privacy, employment, tax, product-safety, intellectual-property, and criminal laws of each applicable jurisdiction.

Legal consequences: A violation of a section may result, where the applicable law permits, in corrective instructions, warnings, contract remedies, account or access restrictions, removal, refunds, restitution, civil damages, injunctions, administrative fines, regulatory orders, seizure, license consequences, or criminal investigation and prosecution. Penalties are not automatic: the governing law, jurisdiction, evidence, defenses, exceptions, intent, harm, due process, statutory limits, and decision of the competent authority control.

Policy clause

Bonds Mall’s e-commerce policy is to conduct commerce honestly, safely, transparently, and with reasonable care for customers, sellers, employees, partners, communities, and the environment. Policies are designed to support lawful trade and consistent service; they do not reduce a right or protection that cannot lawfully be waived.

11.1 Product and listing policy

  • Every listing should identify the product accurately, including the commercial name, brand where known, condition, materials, dimensions, quantity, model or identifier, included items, limitations, images, and material specifications.
  • Images must represent the listed product or be clearly identified as illustrative. Similar-product images, substitutions, mockups, stock photos, and generated images must not be presented as proof of an exact item when they are not.
  • Listings must not contain counterfeit goods, stolen goods, deceptive replicas, unsafe products, unlawful services, prohibited financial instruments, or products restricted by applicable law or marketplace policy.
  • Product categories, attributes, brands, availability, and shipping origin should be maintained accurately so customers can make informed choices and safety notices can reach affected buyers.
  • Sellers must have the right to offer the product and must preserve evidence of authenticity, sourcing, safety, licenses, permits, and supply-chain traceability when applicable.

11.2 Pricing and promotion policy

  • Prices, reference prices, discounts, coupon codes, bundles, subscriptions, taxes, shipping charges, and other fees must be calculated and displayed accurately.
  • A reference or crossed-out price must be genuine, supportable, and presented in a manner that does not create a false impression of savings.
  • Promotions must state material conditions, eligibility, geographic limits, inventory limits, expiration dates, exclusions, and any required redemption steps.
  • Bonds Mall may correct an obvious typographical, data, currency, or calculation error and may cancel or re-price an affected order where permitted by law, with prompt notice and a refund of amounts paid for a cancelled order.
  • Prices may vary by market, currency, tax, seller, inventory, delivery destination, or promotion. The checkout total and applicable terms control for the specific order.

11.3 Advertising, content, and reviews policy

  • Commercial claims should be truthful, supportable, current, and understandable to the intended audience.
  • Paid placements, affiliate links, sponsored content, influencer relationships, comparative claims, endorsements, urgency claims, and customer incentives must be identified where required.
  • Reviews and ratings must reflect genuine experiences. Users may not purchase reviews, suppress material negative feedback, impersonate reviewers, or coordinate manipulation.
  • Users grant only the rights necessary for Bonds Mall to host, display, moderate, and operate with submitted content, subject to the applicable content terms and privacy rules.
  • Do not submit content that infringes intellectual property, violates privacy, contains malware, exposes confidential information, or is unlawful, hateful, harassing, threatening, or abusive.

11.4 Account and identity policy

  • Account information must be accurate, current, and controlled by the person or organization using the account.
  • Users must protect credentials, payment instruments, recovery methods, and authentication codes and must report suspected compromise promptly.
  • One person may not impersonate another person, create deceptive duplicate accounts, evade a restriction, or use another person’s account without authorization.
  • Bonds Mall may request proportionate verification for fraud prevention, age or eligibility checks, seller onboarding, payment security, sanctions screening, or legal compliance.
  • Account restrictions should be documented and reviewed through an appropriate support or appeal route when feasible and legally appropriate.

11.5 Ordering and contract-formation policy

  • A customer should review the item, quantity, price, delivery address, taxes, shipping cost, return terms, and final total before submitting an order.
  • Adding an item to a cart or submitting an order request does not guarantee inventory, price, acceptance, or delivery.
  • An order is accepted only when Bonds Mall or the applicable seller confirms acceptance under the displayed checkout terms. Automated acknowledgments may confirm receipt without confirming acceptance.
  • Orders may be held, declined, limited, or cancelled for lack of stock, payment failure, fraud indicators, address restrictions, legal restrictions, supplier failure, system error, or other lawful business reasons.
  • Where an order is cancelled after payment, the responsible party should initiate a refund or reversal through the original payment method as required by the applicable policy and law.

11.6 Payment and financial-transaction policy

  • Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Bonds Mall should not store full payment credentials when a secure provider can process them instead.
  • Customers may use only payment methods they are authorized to use and must not submit stolen, altered, deceptive, or unauthorized payment information.
  • Payment authorization, capture, settlement, refunds, chargebacks, currency conversion, and financial records may be subject to provider rules and timing outside Bonds Mall’s direct control.
  • Bonds Mall may use reasonable transaction monitoring and verification to reduce fraud, money laundering, account takeover, abuse, and unlawful payments.
  • Customers should contact Bonds Mall before initiating a chargeback when practical, but no policy requires a customer to surrender a lawful right to dispute an unauthorized or improper charge.

11.7 Tax, customs, and cross-border policy

  • Taxes, duties, customs charges, brokerage fees, import restrictions, and delivery requirements may depend on the customer’s location, product, seller, and shipment route.
  • Where required, Bonds Mall or the seller should provide tax information, invoices, receipts, customs data, and legally required seller or importer details.
  • Customers are responsible for providing an accurate delivery destination and for cooperating with lawful customs or delivery requests. The checkout page should identify charges or responsibilities known at the time of order.
  • Cross-border goods must not be shipped to prohibited destinations or in violation of export, import, sanctions, product-safety, or trade-control requirements.

11.8 Shipping, fulfillment, and delivery policy

  • Shipping methods, origins, delivery estimates, tracking availability, handling times, and applicable restrictions should be described accurately.
  • Delivery estimates are estimates unless a binding delivery commitment is expressly stated. Delays may arise from inventory, carriers, weather, customs, public emergencies, address issues, or events outside reasonable control.
  • Sellers and fulfillment partners must package products appropriately, use the agreed carrier or method, provide accurate tracking where available, and respond to lost, damaged, or misdelivered shipment reports.
  • Customers should inspect deliveries promptly, preserve packaging and evidence for damage claims, and report material delivery problems within the period stated in the applicable policy or required by law.
  • Risk of loss, title, delivery responsibility, and signature requirements depend on the order terms, seller arrangement, shipping method, and applicable law.

  • Refunds should identify the amount, method, timing, deductions, shipping treatment, and any required return steps. A refund is not complete until the payment provider or financial institution processes it.
  • Customers must not abuse return, refund, warranty, replacement, or chargeback processes through false claims, serial misuse, product substitution, or concealment of damage.
  • Sellers must honor valid warranties, statutory guarantees, recalls, and remedies that apply to their products and jurisdiction.

  • Partners may not use Bonds Mall data, brand assets, customer lists, product feeds, APIs, or credentials outside the permissions and contract that authorize their use.
  • Bonds Mall may request documents, suspend a listing, hold funds where permitted, or terminate a relationship when information is incomplete, unreliable, unsafe, unlawful, or materially misleading.
  • Nothing in this policy creates an employment, agency, franchise, fiduciary, joint-venture, or exclusive relationship unless a separate written agreement expressly does so.

11.11 Privacy, security, and accessibility policy

  • Personal information should be collected, used, shared, retained, and deleted according to the Privacy Notice, disclosed purposes, user choices, contracts, and applicable law.
  • Teams should use data minimization, access controls, encryption or equivalent safeguards, secure development, vendor review, monitoring, backups, and incident response appropriate to the risk.
  • Customer data must not be used for unrelated marketing, profiling, resale, or disclosure without an appropriate legal basis, notice, consent, contract, or other authorization.
  • Bonds Mall should provide reasonable accessibility support and avoid interfaces, documents, or communications that unnecessarily prevent people from understanding or using essential commerce functions.
  • Security reports should be directed to Customer Service or the designated security contact and should not include unnecessary personal or confidential information.

11.12 Prohibited-commerce policy

Users must not use Bonds Mall to conduct, facilitate, promote, or conceal unlawful or harmful activity. Prohibited activity includes fraud, identity theft, unauthorized payments, counterfeiting, trafficking, stolen goods, weapons or unsafe goods where prohibited, controlled substances, exploitation, money laundering, sanctions evasion, harassment, hate-based abuse, malicious code, deceptive advertising, review manipulation, market manipulation, and attempts to compromise systems or accounts.

11.13 Support, complaints, and dispute policy

  • Customers should provide the order number, relevant dates, product information, requested remedy, and concise supporting evidence when contacting support.
  • Support teams should acknowledge material complaints, preserve records, communicate next steps, avoid retaliation, and escalate safety, privacy, payment, legal, and accessibility issues to the responsible team.
  • Dispute decisions should be based on the listing, order record, communications, payment status, tracking, evidence, applicable policy, and mandatory law.
  • Customers retain any non-waivable rights to contact regulators, payment providers, consumer-protection bodies, courts, arbitrators, or other lawful dispute forums.
