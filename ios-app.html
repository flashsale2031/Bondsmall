Shop Bonds Mall on iOS

Add Bonds Mall to your Home Screen for a full-screen, app-style shopping experience. iOS installs this web app from Safari using the Share menu.

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an upward arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen. If shown, leave Open as Web App enabled, then tap Add.
  4. Tap the Bonds Mall icon on your Home Screen to shop.
Open Bonds Mall Shop on the website

This is an installable web app (PWA), not an App Store download. iOS does not provide a browser install prompt for this flow; use Safari’s Share menu. Shopping and checkout require an internet connection.
