Ad-volume mission math

For each product, the seller planner estimates sales required to reach its assigned financial target, multiplies sales by 200 ads, and divides the resulting ad volume across the target city/town count.

Sales needed = financial target ÷ net yield per sale Ads needed = sales needed × 200 Ads per city/town = ads needed ÷ target city/town count Local planning traffic = 30,000 advertising impressions/location/day

Portfolio planning statement

The seller may assign the full financial goal to one product for sensitivity analysis, or divide the $500B target across products. Product estimates are calculated independently and then summed into an aggregate portfolio requirement. Every mission location receives a consistent 30,000/day modeled advertising-impression average until measured local traffic is available.

Traffic methodology

The 30,000/day figure is a planning average, not a claim that every city or town receives 30,000 vehicles or pedestrians. Platform traffic figures below are published third-party or company-reported benchmarks and are kept separate from the local 30,000/day planning average. This prevents a platform's national traffic from being falsely presented as a city traffic count.
