Ad-volume mission math

For each product, the seller planner estimates the sales required to reach its assigned financial target, multiplies sales by 200 ads, and divides the resulting ad volume across the target city/town count.

Sales needed = financial target ÷ net yield per sale Ads needed = sales needed × 200 Ads per city/town = ads needed ÷ target city/town count
Default financial goal$500B
Default ad ratio200 ads/sale

Portfolio planning statement

The seller may assign the full financial goal to one product for sensitivity analysis, or divide the $500B target across products. Product estimates are calculated independently and then summed into an aggregate portfolio requirement. Per-city/town estimates are based on the number of target cities/towns entered in the Seller mission planner.

Planning assumption

The 200-ad ratio is an operating assumption for planning, not a guaranteed conversion rate. Actual yield can differ because of product price, inventory, refunds, platform limits, geography, ad quality, market demand, and operating costs.
