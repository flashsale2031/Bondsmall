Ad-volume mission math

For each product, the seller planner estimates the sales required to reach its assigned financial target, multiplies sales by 200 ads, and divides the resulting ad volume across the target city/town count.

Sales needed = financial target ÷ net yield per sale Ads needed = sales needed × 200 Ads per city/town = ads needed ÷ target city/town count Modeled daily advertising traffic = 30,000 impressions/location/day

Portfolio planning statement

The seller may assign the full financial goal to one product for sensitivity analysis, or divide the $500B target across products. Product estimates are calculated independently and then summed into an aggregate portfolio requirement. Each target city/town is assigned the same 30,000 daily advertising-impression planning average unless a better local traffic source is later entered.

Traffic methodology

The 30,000/day figure is a planning average, not a claim that every city or town actually receives 30,000 vehicles or pedestrians. It is an advertising-impression benchmark intended to give every location a consistent starting value. FHWA defines ADT as an average of daily traffic counts, while outdoor advertising sources report urban billboard exposure in the tens of thousands of daily impressions. Actual location-level traffic should replace the benchmark whenever a measured or independently sourced figure is available.
