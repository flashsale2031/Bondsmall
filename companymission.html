Ad-volume mission math

For each product, the seller planner estimates sales required to reach its assigned financial target, multiplies sales by 200 ads, and divides the resulting ad volume across the target city/town count.

Sales needed = financial target ÷ net yield per sale Ads needed = sales needed × 200 Ads per city/town = ads needed ÷ target city/town count State profit target = $500B × state population share State location target = 19,500 × state population share Platform state traffic = platform U.S. daily visits × state population share

Traffic methodology

The platform figures are current July 2026 web-traffic benchmarks where available. Craigslist has 119.54M U.S. visits for July; Facebook has 2.38B U.S. visits; Nextdoor has 152.08M U.S. visits; Mercari has 23.01M U.S. visits; and OfferUp's latest surfaced benchmark is 8.50M total visits for June. Each monthly figure is converted to a daily average and then allocated to states by 2025 Census population share. Facebook Marketplace is not separately measurable here, so the Facebook figure is the parent Facebook website, not Marketplace-only traffic.
