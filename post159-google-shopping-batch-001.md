## Evidence standard

For each product, collect at least seven distinct retailer offers from Google Shopping or the linked retailer product pages. Record retailer name, exact product or variant match, observed price, URL, currency, and collection date. Exclude shipping, tax, used/open-box listings, and materially different variants unless explicitly noted.

The **retail price** is derived from the highest verified comparable offer among at least seven retailers. The **sale price** is derived from the lowest verified offer among at least seven discount retailers, provided the offer is a genuine comparable product. Both values are converted to the nearest commercial price ending in `.99`; the rounding must preserve retail above sale and must not force prices into a repeated fixed set such as 29.99, 49.99, 79.99, or 499.99.

Where seven reliable comparable offers cannot be verified, the product remains unchanged and is marked `price_pending` rather than receiving an invented price.
